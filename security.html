Malware and Cyber Threats

The Morris Worm

The Morris Worm was said to be one of the most famous malware attacks. This incident happened on Novemeber 2, 1988 and it was created by Robert Tappan Morris to measure the size of the internet. It was significant becasause it infected thousands of computers across universities and goverments labs which caused around 100 million in damages. This incident led to the first Computer Emergency Response team which would help stop future cyber threats.
